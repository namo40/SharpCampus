The [library section](../libraries/) put one library at a time in the leading role. This section flips that around and leads with one principle at a time. Principles like "never block the tick loop" or "settle it at compile time" belong to no single library — they are design attitudes running across the whole stack, and scattered over individual pages they only show up as fragments. These four essays put the fragments back together.
